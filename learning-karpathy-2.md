## It took me 2 months to get through Video 2 of the Karpathy series (MakeMore Part 1)

Why so long, you wonder?

Well, I only actually studied 4 days out of those 2 months. The rest of the time I was wallowing in self pity, disappearing, and having the occasional existential crisis that usually comes from being a corporate servant for longer than what’s best for one’s health.

#### What did I struggle with?

1. **Consistency** - I would come back at the halfway mark after 20 days and now I don’t remember what a bigram is. Why are we normalizing probabilities row-wise? And well, what is a tensor? (PyTorch reference baby.)
2. **Terms** - I forget what they mean. It’s not like everyday conversations include phrases like “let’s calculate the negative log likelihood to represent model quality”. And visualizing is hard. Most terms are too abstract.
3. **Interest** - I forget why I started in the first place. What do I want to do with this knowledge? Well, I am open to suggestions here from anyone. (HELP! I am doing useless things.)

#### What did I learn?

1. The fact that neural networks somehow work is magical in the same way physical phenomena being defined by differential equations works.
2. From randomness to clarity -> the essence of “training”.
3. There is something philosophical about NNs apart from being extremely mathematical. I wonder if all of existence can one day be defined by creations such as these. Or perhaps the limits we hit will once again confound us. And another winter will begin.
